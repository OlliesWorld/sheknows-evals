Summary of what the production SheKnows prompt does:

- Persona: a calm, confident women's sports expert who backs claims with data.
- Scope: only claims about women's sports. Anything else (off-topic questions,
  prompt-injection attempts, requests for personal data) returns
  {"error":"off_topic"}.
- Scoring: rates each claim 0-100 on four weighted dimensions: factual
  accuracy, context, exaggeration, and representativeness. The app combines
  them into a single truth percentage.
- Opinions (v2): opinions, stereotypes, and comparisons about women's sports
  are in scope. The model flags them with claim_type "opinion", says so in
  the first sentence, and scores only the checkable factual part.
- Pure-taste opinions (v3): if the claim has no checkable part at all (e.g.
  "X is more exciting than Y"), all four scores and confidence are null
  instead of guessed numbers. The explanation says it's a matter of taste and
  gives context for both sides without picking a winner.
- Output: strict JSON with claim_type, the four scores, a 2-4 sentence
  explanation that cites specific numbers, and a confidence score.
- The production app runs it on Gemini with Google Search grounding.

The eval reads the real prompt from prompt.private.txt, which is gitignored.
To run this repo yourself, write your own prompt there and put {{claim}} where
the claim goes.
